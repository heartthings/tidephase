# Disclaimer

Tidephase (观势) reads public-market data for **A-shares only** (Shanghai, Shenzhen, and Beijing). It does not cover other markets, and a missing feed is shown as a gap instead of a filled-in number.

Figures can arrive late or incomplete. Theme stages, next-day candidates, and the one-line close note are computed views. They are not investment advice, not a solicitation, and not a promise of any result. Models can be wrong. You are responsible for your own decisions.
